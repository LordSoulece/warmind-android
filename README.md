# Warmind for Android

The Warmind app for Android, to install straight from your phone's browser. Each release holds the app file, `warmind.apk`.

**Download it and follow the steps at [warmind.online/android](https://warmind.online/android).** The page covers moving from the copy you got from Google Play without losing your lists, and the questions Android asks the first time you install an app from your browser.

Once installed, the app keeps itself up to date.

On an iPhone? [Warmind is on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6784827876). Prefer no install? [Build lists in your browser](https://warmind.online/builder/).
